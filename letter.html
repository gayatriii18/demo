My Love KK❤️🎀,


I hope this letter finds you in good health and high spirits. I wanted to take a moment to express my deepest feelings for you and let you know just how much you mean to me. You are the light of my life, the one who brings joy and happiness into every day. Your smile brightens even the darkest of days, and your voice is music to my ears. 🥰

I am grateful for every moment we share together, and I cherish the love we have built. I promise to always be there for you, to support you through thick and thin, and to love you unconditionally. Thank you for being the amazing person that you are and for filling my life with love and warmth. I look forward to many more beautiful memories with you by my side.😘

And just a casual reminder that you are, hands down, my absolute favourite person to annoy. 👉👈 Thank you for always listening to my endless rants, pretending my terrible jokes are actually funny, and not judging my questionable life choices. You're basically the spice to my biryani, and the only person I'd willingly share the leg piece with (and yk how serious I am about food). That is, of course, when you actually decide to leave your room! I swear, getting you to hang out requires a written application submitted 3-5 business days in advance. Which still get rejected, your excuses deserve an award at this point, but ohk.😂

Also, can we talk about your highly selective memory? You have the brain of a supercomputer. You can remember complex formulas, historical dates, political news but the moment it comes to replying to my texts or remembering our plans, suddenly your brain reverts to factory settings. You claim you "just forget things," but let's be real—that glitch applies only to me!🥱

Honestly, though, despite your broken memory and your permanent residency in the library, I am so incredibly proud of how hard you work. Your focus and dedication are seriously amazing to watch.🙌🏼 I know you're going to absolutely crush your goals, and I'm cheering for you every step of the way (even if I have to throw pebbles at your window to get your attention). i believe in you, study hard,achieve your goals and then reward yourself by taking me out for biryani.😋
I Love You sooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooo much💋🦘🌻✨
